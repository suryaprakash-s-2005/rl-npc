OVERLEAF INSTRUCTIONS FOR CONFERENCE ORGANIZERS
Paper 1323 - Auditing Strategy-Conditioned Experience Replay for Reinforcement Learning Non-Player Characters: A Pre-Registered Negative Result

The ZIP contains the complete LaTeX source, figures, and a compiled reference PDF.

1. Upload the ZIP to Overleaf:
   - Log in to Overleaf.
   - Choose "New Project" > "Upload Project".
   - Select "1323_overleaf.zip" and upload it.

2. Open the project and confirm the main file:
   - Main document: main.tex
   - Figures folder: figures/

3. Select the compiler:
   - Recommended: XeLaTeX (this matches the included compiled PDF).
   - Alternative: pdfLaTeX also works because the source is plain ASCII.

4. Click "Recompile".
   - The output PDF should be 6 pages and include 4 figures, 3 tables, and 27 references.
   - Citation numbers in the text are clickable and jump to the corresponding reference entry.
   - Overleaf will create its own output PDF; the included main.pdf is a reference copy from the same source.

5. Download the generated PDF if needed.
